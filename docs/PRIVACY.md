# Twinwave privacy policy

Updated November 15, 2026.

Twinwave is a local Mac audio utility from CheeseApps. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network service.

With your macOS permission, Twinwave measures microphone and system-output audio levels. It reduces audio buffers to peak measurements in memory; it does not record or save audio files. Its input meter runs while another audio client is using the selected microphone. It reads local audio-device and process properties to detect that activity and display or change your selected system devices. This information stays on your Mac.

Twinwave saves panel placement preferences locally. macOS manages microphone and system-audio permissions; you can revoke them in System Settings.

Support messages you choose to send through the support channel are handled by that service. Do not include sensitive recordings or information in a public support request.

Support: [Twinwave support](https://github.com/XiupengMa/Twinwave/issues). Please avoid posting recordings or sensitive information in public issues.
